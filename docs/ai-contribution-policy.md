# ARKELYTHEX's AI Contribution Policy

## Principles

- **AI is a tool; humans are accountable.** Every contribution has a human author who understands, owns, and answers for it — including AI-assisted code.
- **No AI attribution.** Never add `Co-Authored-By`, "generated by", or similar AI/tool attribution to commits, PRs, or release notes. The human owns the change.
- **AI is advisory, never fiscal authority.** AI prepares and reviews; humans approve material actions. The human accountant remains the final authority.

## AI-assisted contributions

- AI-assisted code must be reviewed by a human before it lands: understand the diff, verify the tests, and be able to explain the change in review.
- AI-assisted code requires tests for business logic, exactly like any other contribution.
- A human must be able to defend the change during review; an unverifiable AI artifact is not acceptable.

## Sensitive domains

- **Fiscal/SUNAT changes require compliance tests with canonical vectors** — regardless of whether AI was involved.
- **Receipts/ledger changes require conformance vectors against the frozen contract** — regardless of whether AI was involved.
- AI output in these domains is treated as a first draft requiring human verification; it is never merged unreviewed.

## AI in review

- Automated review (GGA) complements human review; it never replaces it. A passing automated review is not approval.
- Reviewers hold AI-assisted and human-written contributions to the same standard.

## Administrative checklist

Configure and verify these controls in GitHub Settings or organizational infrastructure as code (IaC), not in `AGENTS.md`, workflow YAML, or source files:

- [ ] Require approval from an independent human reviewer; prevent authors from approving their own PRs. A passing GGA review never replaces human approval.
- [ ] Disable administrator and role-based bypass of review and branch protections; allow no bypass actors.
- [ ] Scope `OPENAI_API_KEY` to the `gga-review` environment and its intended job only.
- [ ] Restrict environment deployments to intended pull requests; operators must verify the restriction works with the chosen event trigger, especially `pull_request_target`.
- [ ] Protect `main`: require PR approval and a successful GGA check, and disallow force-pushes and branch deletion.
